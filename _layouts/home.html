Welcome!

Hi, I'm Vector Nguyen, an AI Engineer specializing in multi-agent systems, LLMs, agentic voice, and production AI infrastructure. I studied Mechatronics Engineering at HCMUTE, then transitioned into AI — first at FPT Software building computer vision and NLP systems, then at FPT's AI Center working on multimodal RAG, multi-agent architectures, and multilingual AI solutions for enterprise clients.

I'm currently an AI Engineer at MoMo, where I designed and built Tro Ly An Choi — a zero-to-one AI chat assistant with 1 orchestrator and 6 domain agents, integrated across 10+ MoMo mini-app surfaces. The system has achieved over 100K monthly engaged users. I built 70+ agent tools covering cinema, airline, bus, train ticketing, and food ordering — all with deep payment integration into MoMo's checkout system.

Outside of work, I build Assistant Core (assistantcore.com) — a platform for deploying one AI assistant everywhere: web, IoT devices, wearables, and embedded widgets, with sub-1s voice pipeline, 7+ AI providers, agentic RAG, and white-label multi-tenant architecture. Feel free to explore my projects or reach out to collaborate!
